# Yuma Training Doctrine

Yuma training favors systems, discipline, industrial leverage, and repeatable procedure.

## Mechanical Expression

Yuma-aligned training should often touch:

- `Precision`
- `Patch`
- `Fabricate`
- `Signal`
- `Suppress`
- `Calibrate`

## Specialty Tone

Yuma-flavored Specialties should feel engineered:

- measured firing lines
- field repairs
- industrial discipline
- doctrine under pressure
- controlled escalation

This does not mean all Yuma characters are rigid. It means their institutions tend to teach mastery as procedure.
