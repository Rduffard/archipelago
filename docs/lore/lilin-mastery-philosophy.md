# Lilin Mastery Philosophy

Lilin mastery treats power as relationship: spirit, memory, oath, pressure, and consequence.

## Mechanical Expression

Lilin-aligned training should often touch:

- `Focus`
- `Ward`
- `Invoke`
- `Attune`
- `Sift`

## Specialty Tone

Lilin-flavored Specialties should feel relational and ritualized:

- listening before acting
- naming hidden currents
- binding costs through ceremony
- reading memory left by power
- turning fear into negotiated meaning

Lilin mastery should never feel like generic spell slots pasted onto the setting.
