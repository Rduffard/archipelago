# Institutional Power Structures

Institutional power is how factions turn belief, violence, labor, ritual, law, and access into campaign pressure. It should shape mechanics without reducing factions to stat packages.

## Design Role

Institutions provide:

- permissions
- training
- sanctuary
- documents
- legitimacy
- restricted knowledge
- witnesses
- protection
- retaliation

They also create obligations, surveillance, compromise, and enemies.

## Yuma Republic

Power expression:

- military hierarchy
- industrial command
- patrol networks
- permits and seizure authority
- disciplined training schools

Mechanical pressure:

- access through rank, paperwork, and visible compliance
- consequences through patrol clocks, warrants, and inspection
- training through doctrine, formation, suppression, and command

Yuma should feel organized, capable, and costly to disobey in public.

## Lilin Empire

Power expression:

- ritual legitimacy
- spirit courts
- oath structures
- imperial mysticism
- inherited ceremonial authority

Mechanical pressure:

- access through ritual recognition and spiritual procedure
- consequences through omen debt, taboo, and formal accusation
- training through warding, attunement, invocation, and relational mastery

Lilin should feel like power that remembers what has been promised.

## Free Captains

Power expression:

- reputation at sea
- hidden harbors
- mutual threat
- crews bound by charisma, fear, or loyalty
- refusal of imperial order

Mechanical pressure:

- access through reputation, daring, and useful defiance
- consequences through betrayal, route denial, and public challenge
- training through boarding, feinting, suppression, and escape

Free Captains should feel liberating and dangerous, never simply lawless.

## Guild Consortium

Power expression:

- craft monopolies
- shipyards
- salvage claims
- patents
- controlled expertise
- trade infrastructure

Mechanical pressure:

- access through debt, licenses, contracts, and proof of competence
- consequences through blacklisting, seized tools, and withheld repair
- training through calibration, fabrication, jury-rigging, and salvage doctrine

The Guild should make logistics feel political.

## Faith Orders

Power expression:

- sanctuary
- doctrine
- sacred witness
- spiritual care
- ritual authority outside imperial law

Mechanical pressure:

- access through piety, service, restraint, and trust
- consequences through shame, exile, taboo, and refused rites
- training through warding, corruption discipline, and oath practice

Faith Orders should complicate power by asking what must not be done.

## Underworld Networks

Power expression:

- secrets
- smuggling
- assassination
- quiet introductions
- black markets
- leverage over respectable institutions

Mechanical pressure:

- access through favors, silence, and proof of discretion
- consequences through exposure, debt, blackmail, and disappearance
- training through guile, brokerage, infiltration, and threat

The Underworld should make access easy and exit expensive.

## Design Constraint

When a faction grants a benefit, document the institution behind it. A reward without an institution is just loot. A reward with an institution becomes story pressure.
