# Faction Unlocks

Faction unlocks connect reputation to training, access, and narrative authority. They should make the character more embedded in the world, not merely stronger.

## Unlock Types

Use faction standing to grant:

- Specialty access
- Specialty rank trainers
- prerequisite reductions
- restricted equipment permissions
- sanctuary or passage
- faction tags on existing techniques
- institutional introductions
- legal, ritual, or underworld authority

## Unlock Requirements

Possible requirements:

- reputation threshold
- completed service
- sponsor
- public oath
- ideological proof
- rival faction cost
- possession of a relevant Core Check value
- prior Specialty rank

Requirements should explain why the faction would teach, trust, or authorize the character.

## Example Unlocks

### Yuma Republic

- unlock `Command` training through officer doctrine
- reduce `Suppress III` prerequisite when acting with a disciplined squad
- grant restricted access to military docks
- create obligation to report rebel movement

### Lilin Empire

- unlock advanced `Attune` or `Ward` instruction
- allow ritual access to spirit courts
- reduce prerequisite for corruption-facing containment techniques
- create obligation to honor spiritual procedure even when slow

### Free Captains

- unlock `Board`, `Feint`, or ambush-oriented training
- provide hidden routes and safe harbors
- reduce training cost for escape or lane-control techniques
- create obligation to refuse certain imperial demands

### Guild Consortium

- unlock `Calibrate`, `Jury-Rig`, or salvage training
- provide prototype tools and repair yards
- reduce prerequisites for craft-linked Utility Specialties
- create obligation around patents, salvage claims, or debt

## Design Constraint

Faction unlocks should always imply who taught the character and who now recognizes that training. If the answer is "no one," the unlock probably belongs to generic milestone progression instead.
