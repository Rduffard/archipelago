# Milestone Progression

Milestone progression records campaign change. It should reward meaningful decisions, survival through pressure, completed obligations, and new authority rather than simple encounter count.

## Milestone Sources

Grant milestones for:

- resolving a major faction conflict
- surviving a dangerous journey with lasting consequence
- completing a training obligation
- making a public ideological choice
- securing or refusing institutional access
- binding, destroying, or bargaining with a relic
- changing the political state of an island or route

## Reward Types

Milestones can grant:

- Core Points
- Specialty Training
- faction unlocks
- Attribute growth in rare transformative moments
- new contacts or sanctuary
- narrative authority over a route, institution, or community
- reduced prerequisite for a clearly justified Specialty rank

## Reward Mix

Use mixed rewards to preserve the layered model.

Example:

After ending a dock blockade, the party might gain:

- 1 Core Point from hard-earned field competence
- access to a Guild trainer for `Calibrate`
- `Rebel Movements +1` and `Yuma Republic -1`
- safe passage through one Free Captain route

The reward advances capability, identity, and politics at the same time.

## Attribute Milestones

Attribute increases should be rare because they alter root identity.

Use them when:

- a character survives a transformation
- an oath changes how they carry themselves
- a faction remakes their discipline
- a relic exposure permanently marks them
- a long campaign arc redefines their nature

Do not grant Attribute increases as routine level-up rewards.

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- milestone rewards that only increase numbers
- generic treasure as the default advancement engine
- progression that ignores faction consequence
- rewards that make all characters converge mechanically
- advancement with no explanation in the fiction
