# Dazibinian Principality

8. Dazibinan Principality — Sky Kingdom of Blimps
   Theme: Innovation + inexperience, steampunk naïveté
   Inspiration: Studio Ghibli airship cities
   Core Concept
   A young prince rules a vibrant, blimp-powered steampunk principality.
   Traits

- Airship fleets

- Conspirators everywhere

- The prince is a figurehead — nobility actually pull strings

Story Themes

- Coup attempts

- Airborne heists

- Choosing to support or overthrow the prince

---

🌥️ GEOGRAPHY & CLIMATE
Dazibinany is an island that feels like it’s floating even when you’re on land.
Distinct Features:

- Towering white cliffs rising straight from the sea

- Rolling green hills dotted with windmills

- Valleys filled with mist each morning

- Skyship docks built into cliff faces

- A massive central plateau where their capital hangs above the valley on suspension bridges

- Unique mineral deposits that burn clean and are used for lighter-than-air engines

## The entire island smells faintly of oil, ozone, and fresh wind.

⏳ BEFORE THE PIRATE KING
Dazibinany was originally a scattered set of hill clans and farming communities.
Everything changed when:

- A brilliant engineer, Tovran Daz, discovered “aetherflame gas” in the cliffs

- Balloons and primitive airships became possible

- The clans unified under shared technological innovation

They quickly became:

- the first island with sky travel

- the primary messenger network of the Archipelago

- a hub for inventors and dreamers

But power moved faster than politics.
Even before the Pirate King, noble factions competed for influence over the fledgling principality.

---

👑 UNDER THE PIRATE KING
The Pirate King adored Dazibinany’s ingenuity.
He:

- protected their independence

- encouraged airship trade

- sent craftsmen from Klechi to collaborate

- commissioned three skyships of his own

- visited frequently, enjoying the island’s optimism

He and the ruling family were on excellent terms.
His final visit here included hours of discussion with the prince’s father — with no witnesses.
After the Pirate King's death, the old prince died mysteriously within a year.
His child took the throne.

---

⚔️ AFTER THE PIRATE KING’S DEATH (Years 1–10)
Now the island is ruled by his son:
Prince Elrick Dazibinany — “The Boy in the Sky”
Only in his early twenties.
A kind, brilliant, naïve young ruler.
He’s beloved by the common folk and idealists.
And targeted by every noble with ambition.
Under his reign:

- Skyship production increased

- Trade expanded

- The island became a symbol of hope

But behind the scenes:

- Sabotage threatens the docks

- Nobles plot coups

- Foreign emissaries try to manipulate the prince

- A mysterious “aether sickness” affects some skyfarers

- The prince’s advisors quietly undermine him

Dazibinany smiles in the sun…
but storms gather above.

---

🛡️ CURRENT POLITICAL STATE

1. Prince Elrick — Idealistic Ruler
   A youth with:

- a brilliant engineering mind

- a compassionate heart

- an inability to recognize danger

He wants peace, innovation, and unity among the islands.
He dreams of an Archipelago “connected through the skies.”
His fatal flaw:
He trusts too easily.

---

2. The Windwright Council (Real Power Brokers)
   A group of powerful nobles, engineers, and industrialists.
   They control:

- skyship production

- fuel mining

- aetherflame refinement

- the skyport economy

Publicly, they support the prince.
Privately, they consider him a puppet.
Their leader, Lady Maivra Sorelle, is cunning, composed, and utterly loyal—to herself.

---

3. The Aetherguard
   Elite aerial soldiers trained to:

- board enemy ships mid-air

- fight using wind-assisted techniques

- operate gliders and jet-assisted packs

They defend the principality from above.
But morale is split:

- Some are loyal to the prince

- Some follow the Windwright Council

- Some may be bought by outsiders

---

4. The Undersky Movement (Revolutionaries)
   A growing faction among the poor:

- Mine workers

- Dock loaders

- Scrappers

- Pilots left injured from test flights

They believe the Windwright Council exploits them.
They want social reform—and the prince’s attention.
Some want peaceful change.
Some want blood.

---

5. The Fogcallers
   A secretive group of mystics descended from the old hill clans.
   They claim that:

- the island’s recent “aether disturbances”

- malfunctioning skyships

- unusual weather

- strange lights in the clouds

are warnings.
They believe a great disaster may come from the skies.

---

🧩 KEY LOCATIONS
☁️ Cloudspire City (Capital)
Built on a high plateau connected by suspension bridges and skyrails.
Filled with:

- airship hangars

- rotating windmills

- elegant ropewalks

- academies for aeronautics

- bustling sky markets

## It feels vibrant and airy — but shadows hide in walkways beneath the platforms.

🎈 The Grand Skyport
The largest airship dock in the world.
Home to:

- trade fleets

- luxury sky yachts

- pirate sky vessels

- experimental prototypes

## Sabotage is becoming a serious problem.

⚙️ The Aetherflame Mines
Deep cliffside caverns containing the mineral that made Dazibinany famous.
But miners report:

- vibrating stone

- glowing veins

- strange humming sounds

- vanished workers

## Something is wrong underground.

🌀 The Tempest Reach
A dangerous upper-atmosphere zone where powerful storms brew.
Aether scholars believe the Pirate King ventured here once — and brought something back.
Some skyfarers won’t fly near it.

---

🏚️ The Broken Lab of Tovran Daz
The ancestral workshop of the island’s founder.
Now sealed.
Rumors say the original inventor was working on a skyship that doesn’t need aetherflame…
a ship that could reach heights no human has survived.
The Pirate King visited here too.

---

🎭 MAJOR NPCS
Prince Elrick Dazibinany
Bright, hopeful, tragic.
He wants to change the world—and doesn’t understand how dangerous that is.
He could become:

- a beloved ally

- a betrayed martyr

- a ruler strengthened by the player

## Your choices shape his fate.

Lady Maivra Sorelle — Windwright Council Leader
Elegant, brilliant, ruthless.
She believes:
“The skies belong to those who build them, not those who dream of them.”
She may:

- support you

- recruit you

- betray you

- attempt a coup

## Her smile hides knives.

Tamsen Gale — Aetherguard Captain
Disciplined, loyal, compassionate but torn.
She sees the truth:

- the prince is too innocent

- the council too greedy

- the island drifting toward catastrophe

## She could become a key companion.

Rion “Dustdrift” Varr — Undersky Leader
Charismatic, angry, beloved by the poor.
He wants justice, not chaos.
Unless pushed.

---

Elyra Mistborn — Fogcaller Mystic
Soft-spoken, eerie.
She claims to have spoken with something in the upper storms.
And it spoke back.

⚔️ QUEST HOOKS

1. “Sabotage in the Sky”
   A skyship explodes during a festival.

- Accident?

- Council plot?

- Undersky retaliation?

- Something… stranger?

## The player investigates.

2. “Lessons for a Young Prince”
   Elrick asks the player to teach him:

- combat

- leadership

- negotiation

## This questline determines the man he becomes.

3. “The Aether Sickness”
   Skyfarers fall ill.
   Is it:

- a tainted aetherflame vein?

- sabotage?

- a previously unknown property of the fuel?

- connected to the Pirate King’s final journey?

---

4. “The Fogcallers’ Warning”
   Elyra reveals a prophecy:
   “When the sky wounds itself, the Archipelago will bleed.”
   This is tied to a major late-game arc.

---

5. “Coup of the Cloudspire”
   The Windwright Council moves to depose the prince.
   The player must choose:

- defend him

- join the coup

- let the island fracture

## This shapes the political landscape of the entire world.

🧭 PLAYER IMPACT
Dazibinany teaches the player:

- the power of innovation

- the fragility of youth in leadership

- the danger of unchecked ambition

- the political volatility of high technology

It’s a place of wonder and hope…
…but also of:

- betrayal

- zealotry

- impending disaster from the skies

Everything here pushes the player closer to the question:
**“If even the brightest hope can be corrupted…
who is left to lead the Archipelago?”**
