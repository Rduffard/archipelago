# Dengz Guo

6. Dengz Guo — The Walled Nation of Gunpowder
   Theme: Discipline, secrecy, ancient power
   Inspiration: Qing Dynasty, massive fortress-cities
   Core Concept
   A heavily fortified island with monumental walls. They perfected gunpowder and artillery long before Yuma did.
   Traits

- Cannon foundries

- Black powder temples

- Firework-coded communications

- Strict martial law

Story Themes

- Breaking into the walled city

- Gunpowder conspiracies

- Forbidden technology

---

🌍 GEOGRAPHY & DESIGN
Dengz Guo is one of the most visually striking islands in the Archipelago.
Geographical Highlights

- A colossal, winding Great Wall encircles the central territory

- Tiered rice terraces that shimmer emerald

- Towering cliffs that house ancient fortresses

- A massive crater lake created by an explosion centuries ago

- Misty bamboo forests hiding training grounds

- Hidden black-powder caves deep within the mountains

The air is often hazy with incense or the faint scent of gunpowder.
This is a land that feels ancient—yet ready for war.

---

⏳ BEFORE THE PIRATE KING
Dengz Guo was once an isolated nation with a strict mandate:
“Let no foreign foot tread our soil.”
This was enforced through:

- elite martial orders

- overwhelming ranged weaponry

- fierce border patrols

- a culture of military excellence

Their mastery of gunpowder predates both Yuma and Lilan.
They discovered it, refined it, weaponized it, and ritualized it.
Every explosion is seen as a prayer to the ancestors.
But isolation created stagnation.
Old traditions became rigid.
The world moved on while Dengz Guo looked inward.

---

👑 UNDER THE PIRATE KING
The Pirate King visited Dengz Guo only once.
Legend says he climbed the Great Wall alone, unarmed.
The Emperor met him personally.
No one knows what was said—only that:

- Dengz Guo ceased firing on foreign ships

- They allowed limited trade

- The Emperor bowed to the Pirate King before he left

This moment is still considered sacrilege among purist elders.
But the Emperor saw the Pirate King as a necessary balancing force.
For ten years, Dengz kept their autonomy.

---

⚔️ AFTER THE PIRATE KING’S DEATH (Years 1–10)
With the Pirate King gone, Dengz Guo hardened again.
They:

- rebuilt their walls

- expanded their military

- doubled production of gunpowder

- banned foreign entry

- began training new elite troops

Their new doctrine:
“A storm is coming. The Vale must be shielded by fire.”
The Archipelago wonders:
Shielded from who?
Yuma? Lilan? Pirates?
Or something far, far older?

---

🛡️ CURRENT POLITICAL STATE

1. The Fire Emperor Wu Zhen (Ruler)
   A stoic, disciplined man.

- Obsessed with preserving Dengz Guo’s strength

- Believes the Pirate King’s death will lead to a continent-wide war

- Preparing Dengz to become the fortress of the world

- Practices ancestral rituals with gunpowder flames

He respects tradition but is not blinded by it.
He is not evil.
But his fear will drive him to extreme measures.

---

2. The Ashen Court (Advisors)
   A council of elders and generals responsible for:

- strategy

- arcane-gunpowder fusion research

- external policy

Half are isolationists.
Half want Dengz Guo to seize control of the Archipelago.

---

3. The Firelance Legion
   Elite soldiers armed with:

- explosive-tipped spears

- gunpowder sabers

- smoke-bomb techniques

- disciplined phalanx tactics

They undergo brutal training in the Shattered Fields—
a volcanic plain where gunpowder is tested.

---

4. The Lantern Scholars
   Alchemists and researchers.
   Their goals:

- refine gunpowder

- fuse magic with explosives

- study ancient crater ruins

- preserve historical records

## Some fear their experiments may unearth something catastrophic.

5. The Silk Rebellion (Secret Insurgents)
   A growing resistance within Dengz Guo:

- scholars

- farmers

- minor nobles

- idealists

They believe the Emperor is driving the nation into stagnation.
They want open borders, cultural exchange, and diplomatic integration.
They work in shadows—silk masks hiding their faces.

---

🧩 KEY LOCATIONS
🔥 The Great Fire Wall
A massive defensive structure enhanced with:

- fire traps

- explosive projectiles

- watchtowers manned day and night

The top level is restricted to elite guards and the Emperor.
A secret:
There is a second wall beneath the ground, older than Dengz civilization.

---

💥 The Shattered Fields
An explosive testing ground where:

- craters glow with ember dust

- the air tastes metallic

- lightning sometimes ignites stray powder

- trainees are pushed to their limits

The Pirate King once fought a duel here.
The crater still smolders.

---

🏯 Emberpeak Palace
The Emperor’s citadel.
Surrounded by:

- smoke gardens

- dragon statues carved from basalt

- scroll archives

- an armory holding legendary explosive relics

The palace lights glow red at night, earning the name:
“The Palace of Eternal Dawn.”

---

📜 The Lantern Archive
A vast underground library housing:

- ancient gunpowder formulas

- blueprints for siege weapons

- journals of imperial ancestors

- documents the Emperor forbids scholars from reading

## The archive holds knowledge the world is not ready for.

💧 The Crater Lake of Ming-Tao
Created centuries ago by a mysterious explosion.
The water is unnaturally warm, shimmering with pale blue light.
Some scholars say the Pirate King retrieved something from its depths.

---

🎭 MAJOR NPCS
Emperor Wu Zhen — The Fire Emperor
Dignified. Calculating. Burdened.
He fears what is coming, but will not admit what he knows.
He may be an ally—if convinced of your strength.
Or a powerful adversary who sees you as a threat to stability.

---

General Liang Fei — Commander of the Firelance Legion
A master strategist.
Dreams of:

- expanding Dengz influence

- breaking isolation

- claiming the Archipelago for “protection”

## He is more dangerous politically than militarily.

Scholar Mei Lian — Lantern Scholar
Brilliant, curious, rebellious.
She uncovered:

- records of the Pirate King’s visit

- forbidden references to “The Old Flame”

- hints of a prophecy only Dengz recorded

## She may become a key ally or companion.

Yeow Rin — Silk Rebellion Leader
Masked leader of the insurgents.
Seeks:

- reform

- diplomacy

- dethroning the Emperor without bloodshed

Their identity is a major plot twist—
possibly a royal relative.

---

⚔️ QUEST HOOKS

1. “The Fire Emperor’s Test”
   The player must pass ceremonial trials involving:

- discipline

- precision

- controlled explosions

Success earns the Emperor’s respect.
Failure earns permanent exile.

---

2. “Secrets Beneath the Wall”
   Rumors of an ancient wall hidden underground reveal:

- something older than Dengz civilization

- the Pirate King investigated it

- the Emperor fears what lies beneath

## This ties into the overarching plot of the Archipelago.

3. “The Silk Mask’s Whisper”
   The rebels recruit the player to infiltrate Emberpeak Palace.
   The player must choose:

- loyalty to the Emperor

- or support for reform

- or play both sides

---

4. “The Crater’s Echo”
   Strange tremors ripple through the Shattered Fields.
   Lantern Scholars fear the crater is awakening.
   What lies at its core?

---

5. “A Flame for the Future”
   The Emperor makes a proposition:
   “The Archipelago will fall into chaos unless a ruler strong enough to unite it rises.
   Tell me… is that ruler you?”
   One of the earliest major “Pirate King aspirant” moments.

---

🧭 PLAYER IMPACT
Dengz Guo forces the player to confront:

- the burden of leadership

- the cost of maintaining power

- whether strength is defined by force, wisdom, or restraint

- the reality that the Pirate King had dealings far more mysterious than anyone knows

This island adds depth to the political structure of the Archipelago and hints at ancient forces tied to the Pirate King's rise — and fall.
