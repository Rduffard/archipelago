# Grand Duchy of Tetlanco

3. Grand Duchy of Tetlanco — The New Monarchs
   Theme: Political rebirth, legitimacy struggles, opportunism
   Core Concept
   After the Pirate King’s death, a powerful noble family claimed rulership and attempted to impose order.
   Traits

- Highly structured feudal courts

- Growing military

- Subtle cracks in legitimacy: usurpers whisper, nobles defect

Story Themes

- Court intrigue

- Succession crisis

- Treason and rebellion

---

🌍 GEOGRAPHY & CLIMATE
Tetlanco is an island of rolling gold-green plains, high plateaus, and fertile vineyards, contrasted by:

- A glittering central river (the River Telan)

- Carved stone terraces

- Two major fortified cities

- Old fort ruins from ancient Lilan attempts at occupation

Tetlanco looks peaceful at a distance—elegant hills, soft colors, flowers in the wind.
Up close, you see soldiers everywhere.

---

⏳ BEFORE THE PIRATE KING
Tetlanco was a fractured cluster of minor baronies:

- Petty nobles

- Constant skirmishes

- Occasional subjugation by Yuma and Lilan fleets

- A culture defined by honor duels, feuding families, and territorial pride

## The island never unified under a single banner.

👑 UNDER THE PIRATE KING
The Pirate King brought one thing Tetlanco never had: stability.
He didn’t conquer it directly — instead:

- He forced the barons into a non-aggression pact

- He established Tetlanco as a protected middle trade stop

- He allowed noble prestige to flourish as long as they didn’t cause trouble

- He visited yearly to ensure cooperation

## The nobles resented his authority but enjoyed the prosperity he brought.

⚔️ AFTER THE PIRATE KING’S DEATH (Years 1–10)
With his death came opportunity — and blood.
In the power vacuum:

- Baronial disputes resurfaced

- Old grudges reignited

- Two baron houses were annihilated

- Yuma and Lilan both attempted covert interference

Then came Duchess Calithra Oretell.
Brilliant, cold, ambitious.
She unified the baronies in less than a decade through:

- Marriages

- Duels

- Strategic bribery

- Quiet assassinations

She crowned herself Grand Duchess, proclaiming the “Birth of Tetlanco.”
Now she rules with beauty, ruthlessness, and intoxicating charisma.

---

👑 CURRENT POLITICAL STATE

1. Grand Duchess Calithra Oretell (Absolute Ruler)
   Her rule is marked by:

- Expanding fortifications

- Aggressive taxation

- A new “Royal Fleet Initiative”

- Banned pirate flags

- Secret negotiations with Yuma

- Intensive spying among her own nobles

Tetlanco is prosperous — but uneasy.
Calithra claims the islands must move beyond piracy.
Many suspect she wants far more:
The Archipelago itself.

---

2. The Tetlanco Noble Court (The Rose Court)
   A nest of intrigue:

- Gossip as weaponry

- Factioned noble families

- “Accidental” duels

- Secret alliances

- Courtiers who smile with poisoned lips

The Rose Court is stunning to visit…
and lethal to remain in.

---

3. The River Guard
   The Duchy’s elite military force:

- Trained in spear-and-shield formations

- Known for their silver-plated helms

- Enforce duchess law across the island

- Often tasked with suppressing pirate sympathizers

They admire their duchess’s strength —
and operate with frightening efficiency.

---

4. The Whispered Blades (Secret Police)
   Calithra’s personal shadow enforcers.
   Identifiable by nothing—
   they don’t exist on paper.
   Rumors:

- They assassinate rebellious nobles

- They suppress evidence about Calithra’s rise

- They hunt anyone claiming the Pirate King’s legacy

- Their leader once served the Pirate King himself

## This last rumor is the most dangerous of all.

🧩 KEY LOCATIONS
🌹 Rosefall Citadel (Capital)
A breathtaking white-marble palace surrounded by:

- Hanging rose gardens

- Crystal fountains

- Ornate dueling rings

- Grand halls used for diplomacy and espionage

## From here, Calithra controls the island with velvet-gloved hands.

⚖️ The Dueler’s Terrace
A dramatic clifftop arena where:

- Nobles settle disputes

- Champions rise to fame

- Outsiders can earn prestige—or offend the wrong people

## Blood spilled here is considered “sacred justice.”

🌾 Veloren Fields
Endless golden plains used for:

- Strategic military training

- Farming

- Secret military testing of new cavalry tactics

## Occasionally, an entire camp disappears overnight…

🛶 The River Telan
A crucial trade artery guarded fiercely by Tetlanco forces.
Rumor:
A hidden underwater vault exists beneath its deepest bend — something the Pirate King once visited.

---

🎭 MAJOR NPCS
Grand Duchess Calithra Oretell

- Strikingly intelligent

- Politically unmatched

- Tragic backstory involving her sister (unknown cause)

- Seeks power “to prevent chaos”—but may become a tyrant

- Knows something about the Pirate King’s fall that no one else does

## She is a major long-term character, possibly a rival or uneasy ally.

Lord Fenrick Ralst (Noble of the Rose Court)

- Charming

- Plays both sides

- Wants Calithra gone

- Will hire the player for secret missions

- Could become an ally — or a traitor

---

Captain Seln Mara (Leader of the River Guard)

- Loyal to Tetlanco above all

- Hates pirates

- Secretly questions Calithra’s extreme tactics

- Might join or oppose the player depending on choices

---

Isha Veylan (Whispered Blade Operative)

- Silent

- Deadly

- Knows the Pirate King personally in the past

- Has a hidden connection to a major plot artifact

## Her interest in the player is… unsettling.

⚔️ QUEST HOOKS

1. “The Duel at Dawn”
   A noble challenges the player to a duel due to a political misunderstanding.
   Winning earns respect.
   Losing earns debts.
   Refusing earns enemies.

---

2. “Letters in Bloom”
   Uncover forged letters meant to provoke a civil war between baronies.

---

3. “The Duchess’s Offer”
   Calithra invites the player to Rosefall Citadel.
   She states calmly:
   “The Archipelago needs a ruler who is not afraid of the crown’s weight.”
   This moment plants a seed:
   What if you could become that ruler?

---

4. “The Whispered Blade”
   After saving or helping Calithra, the player is targeted by assassins.
   But then… one assassin spares you, leaving a cryptic message.

---

5. “Pirate Blood, Noble Honor”
   The player must choose whether to protect a noble child discovered to be secretly born from pirate lineage.
   This decision echoes for the rest of the game.

---

🧭 PLAYER IMPACT
Tetlanco shapes the player's understanding of political ambition.
This is where the player begins to see:

- How power can be seized

- How rulership works

- How one person can unite an island

- How fear and admiration shape nations

- How the Pirate King’s vacuum created monarchs instead of leaders

Tetlanco becomes a key region in later acts — a place that may:

- Support the player’s rise

- Declare you a pirate outlaw

- Attempt to use you

- Attempt to kill you

depending on your choices.
