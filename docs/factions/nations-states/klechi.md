# Klechi

2. Klechi — Island of Shipwrights (Water-7 vibes)
   Theme: Engineering marvel, guild power, isolationism
   Inspiration: Water 7, Venice, Renaissance workshops
   Core Concept
   A legendary city of canals and drydock cities. After the Pirate King’s death, a shipwright elite seized control.
   Traits

- Massive fortified sea walls

- Some of the best ships in the Emerald Vale

- Neutral but armed to the teeth

- Shipwright guild politics, labor strikes, saboteurs

Story Themes

- Gaining a ship

- Naval upgrades

- Helping or opposing guild leadership

- Blueprint heists

---

🌊 GEOGRAPHY & DESIGN
Klechi is a turquoise, sea-washed island built almost entirely around its ship-crafting culture. The island’s topography is uniquely shaped:

- A crescent-shaped harbor perfect for docking hundreds of ships

- Floating drydocks and canal districts

- Massive stone breakwaters engineered to turn even violent storms

- A central lagoon filled with workshops, cranes, and timber mills

Architecture is reminiscent of Venice meets Water 7 meets Renaissance guild cities:

- Marble bridges and aqueducts

- Colorful shipyards rising from the water

- Whale-oil lanterns lighting canals at night

- Clockwork lifts transporting hulls and masts

## The entire city is a tribute to naval mastery.

🏴‍☠️ BEFORE THE PIRATE KING
Klechi was always known for its craftsmen, but for centuries:

- Yuma and Lilan constantly tried to occupy it

- Shipwright guilds were taxed into poverty

- Skilled laborers fled to other islands

## Shipbuilding was a desperate industry, surviving only through stubborn pride.

👑 UNDER THE PIRATE KING
Everything changed.
The Pirate King:

- Declared Klechi a sovereign island

- Offered protection in exchange for maintenance of his fleet

- Personally befriended the Master Shipwright

- Funneled wealth into the guilds

- Encouraged experimentation and innovation

Klechi thrived.
It built the ships that made his legend possible.
To many Klechians, the Pirate King wasn’t a tyrant—
he was the patron saint of their craft.

---

⚔️ AFTER THE PIRATE KING’S DEATH (Years 1–10)
Klechi, knowing it would be a target, acted fast:

- Closed borders

- Reinforced breakwalls

- Built a defensive fleet of shipwright-run vessels

- Unveiled the Klechi Shield, a massive tidal gate that can seal the island completely

Because of this, neither Yuma nor Lilan successfully invaded the island.
Now it stands as the neutral powerhouse of the Archipelago:
the island everyone needs but no one can conquer.

---

🛡️ CURRENT POLITICAL STATE

1. The Shipwright Sovereign (De Facto Ruler)
   Master Shipwright Calder Vox leads the island.

- Brilliant

- Gruff

- Former apprentice of the Pirate King's personal craftsman

- Hates politics

- Loves ships more than people

- Is hiding a massive secret project

## He is respected, feared, and begrudgingly obeyed.

2. The Five Great Guilds
   Klechi’s real political power lies in five guilds who constantly compete and collaborate:
1. Timberwrights Guild

- Controls lumber, sawmills, mast forests

- Oldest guild

2. Ironbell Guild

- Forgers of nails, cannons, anchors, gears

- Militaristic, ambitious

3. Waterglass Guild

- Engineers of canal systems, pumps, hydraulics

- Responsible for flood defenses

4. Tideborne Carvers

- Elite shipwright artisans

- Build luxury vessels, pirate flagships, and unique designs

5. Sparrowhawk Guild

- Spies, scouts, information brokers

- Pretend to be simple navigators

Together, they form the Council of Hull and Harbor.
They rarely agree on anything.

---

3. The Underwake
   A criminal syndicate of smugglers and pirates who operate in Klechi’s shadows.
   Their leader, Sura “Softstep” Lyn, has connections to nearly every island.
   She maintains a fragile truce with Calder Vox.

---

🧩 KEY LOCATIONS
🔧 The Great Dryworks
A colossal multi-level shipyard where:

- Galleons

- Clippers

- Trading barges

- Pirate vessels

are constructed side-by-side.
A labyrinth of scaffolds, wooden beams, lifts, and swings.
In the center lies the Master Dock, where the Pirate King’s ship was once repaired.
Some say it still holds scorch marks from his final voyage.

---

🌊 The Klechi Shield
A marvel of engineering:

- A giant tidal gate

- Reinforces the island against attacks

- Powered by a steam-and-water-pressure system

- Takes 2 hours to fully open, 10 minutes to slam shut

## Yuma desperately wants this technology. Lilan considers it heretical.

🪶 Sparrowhawk Roost
A tall lookout tower housing scouts, cartographers, and spies.
Mapmakers provide navigation to both pirates and legitimate merchants—for a price.

---

💧 The Floodlines
A lower district prone to controlled flooding, filled with taverns, floating shops, and river markets.
When the tide rises, the streets turn into waterways.
During festivals, the whole district becomes a glowing parade of lantern-lit boats.

---

🎭 MAJOR NPCS
Calder Vox — Master Shipwright

- Genius engineer

- Loyal to the Pirate King’s memory

- Stubborn, emotionally closed

- Has a paternal warmth he disguises as annoyance

- His secret: he's building a ship unlike any the world has ever seen

He may be your ally—
or the one trying to stop you from claiming the Pirate King's mantle.

---

Sura Lyn — Queen of the Underwake

- Charismatic

- Manipulative

- Morally flexible

- Controls Klechi’s shadow network

- Knows every illegal shipment that comes through

Rumors say she once loved the Pirate King.
She won’t talk about it.

---

Rilven Pask — Guildmaster of Ironbell

- Militarist

- Wants Klechi to conquer, not hide

- Secretly selling weapons to Yuma and Lilan both

## His ambition could start a war.

⚔️ QUEST HOOKS

1. “A Ship of One’s Own”
   The player attempts to secure their first real vessel by:

- Helping a guild

- Winning a shipwright competition

- Recovering stolen plans

- Or stealing a nearly finished ship

## This becomes a defining early-game quest.

2. “The Underwake Bargain”
   Sura offers the player:

- Rare materials

- Forbidden tech

- Or illegal passage out of Klechi

## …in exchange for a favor that will have consequences later.

3. “The Last Dock the Pirate King Touched”
   Calder reveals knowledge about the Pirate King’s final repairs—
   and hints that he entrusted his final secret to someone from Klechi.

---

4. “The Waterglass Catastrophe”
   The flood-control system is sabotaged.
   Entire districts flood.
   Guilds accuse each other.
   Player must prevent Klechi from tearing itself apart.

---

5. “Iron for Blood”
   The player can expose or assist Ironbell’s war profiteering.
   This affects how Yuma and Lilan view Klechi.

---

🧭 PLAYER IMPACT
Klechi is where the player:

- earns their first major ship

- learns deep world politics

- meets engineers, spies, and pirates

- first hears whispers that someone must take the Pirate King’s place

- chooses whether to align with lawful, neutral, or criminal influences

- sees the consequences of creation being used for war

It’s a major hub with long-term narrative ripples.
