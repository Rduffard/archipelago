# Khiz

5. Khiz — The Water Lords’ Domain (Desert Island)
   Theme: Resource scarcity, tyranny through necessity
   Inspiration: Alabasta, Immortan Joe
   Core Concept
   An arid island where water is life — and one tyrant controls every drop.
   Traits

- Underground reservoirs

- Water taxes

- Droughts, sandstorms

- Rebellion simmering in slums

- Corrupt mercenaries enforcing ration laws

Story Themes

- Revolution

- Sabotaging water stores

- Liberating the oppressed

---

🌍 GEOGRAPHY & CLIMATE
Khiz is an arid island defined by scorching winds and shifting dunes.
It has:

- Heat that cracks stone

- Massive sandstone mesas

- Underground caves holding the last remnants of fresh water

- Occasional oases guarded like holy ground

- Dust-choked canyons perfect for ambushes

Rainfall is nearly nonexistent.
Water is life — and life is controlled.

---

⏳ BEFORE THE PIRATE KING
Khiz was a difficult land, but not always tyrannical.
Once, the island was ruled by the Khizari Clans, a loose coalition of desert tribes who:

- Shared water resources

- Traded with northern islands

- Practiced strict hospitality rituals

Water was sacred, not commodified.
But famine and drought struck.
Clans grew desperate.
War began.

---

👑 UNDER THE PIRATE KING
The Pirate King intervened in one of the bloodiest clan wars.
He did something unprecedented:

- He dug deep into the central mesa

- Found an ancient underground river

- Forced the clans to stop killing each other

- Established a “Water Peace Treaty”

- Left a trusted lieutenant to oversee the island

For ten years, Khiz prospered…
until the Pirate King died.

---

⚔️ AFTER THE PIRATE KING’S DEATH (Years 1–10)
The lieutenant who once administered fair distribution seized absolute control.
His name is Lord Arikan Draath, now known as:
THE WATER LORD
He:

- Took control of the aquifers

- Ended the Water Peace Treaty

- Turned access to water into a weapon

- Built an army loyal to him through dependence

- Crushed clan uprisings

- Became a brutal, beloved despot

Some say he drinks water while watching people beg.
Others say he rations himself to “share their suffering.”
Both cannot be true.

---

🛡️ CURRENT POLITICAL STATE

1. The Water Lord Arikan Draath
   A magnetic tyrant.

- Charismatic

- Brutal

- Genius-level strategic thinker

- Sees himself as a savior, not a dictator

- Blames the Pirate King for “abandoning” Khiz

Wears a mask of dried desert reeds covering half his face.
His voice is soft — the kind that makes people afraid to breathe.
Many follow him willingly.
He keeps Khiz alive… even as he suffocates it.

---

2. The Oasis Guard
   Elite warriors who protect the water caches.
   Their power:

- Spear-and-shield desert combat

- Sand-sense tracking

- Fanatical loyalty to Arikan

## They believe the Water Lord is chosen by the desert itself.

3. The Sandriven Rebellion
   A clandestine resistance group.
   Led by Sahira Vey, granddaughter of the last Khizari Clan Elder.
   They:

- Hide in sandstone caverns

- Map water veins

- Sabotage Oasis Guard caravans

- Smuggle water to the desperate

Their symbol is a broken water jug.
Their goal is not just freedom—
but restoration of the Water Peace Treaty.

---

4. The Thirsting Masses
   Ordinary citizens reduced to:

- bartering everything for water

- accepting oppression to survive

- clinging to rumors of liberation

## Their eyes tell everything: hope hurts.

🧩 KEY LOCATIONS
💧 The Lifewell Citadel
Arikan’s stronghold.
Built atop the deepest aquifer in Khiz.
Contains:

- water silos

- purification chambers

- his throne room carved into stone

- torture chambers for rebels

## Lit by the shimmering blue of reflected water.

🏜️ The Red Wastes
A sun-blasted desert haunted by sandstorms.
Used as:

- a graveyard

- an exile zone

- a battleground

## Buried ruins occasionally emerge when storms shift the dunes — containing relics of pre-drought civilization.

🏺 The Oasis of First Light
The only natural oasis left.
Arikan converted it into a place of worship:

- People kneel to drink

- Water is rationed drop by drop

- Ceremonies insist Arikan is the “chosen one”

## A sacred place corrupted.

🕳️ The Whispering Canyons
A labyrinth of narrow ravines.
Sandriven rebels use it as a hideout.
Their hidden bases change weekly to avoid detection.
Sound travels strangely here — whispers echo like screams.

---

⛓️ The Drip Caves
Natural caverns that drip one drop of water per minute.
Used as prisons.
Prisoners lose sanity counting drops.

---

🎭 MAJOR NPCS
Arikan Draath — The Water Lord
One of the most morally complex villains in the Archipelago.
He genuinely believes:
“Only through fear can Khiz survive.”
Backstory:

- Once a kind administrator

- Lost his family to a water riot

- Watched innocence drowned in desperation

- Decided only tyranny could save Khiz

## He sees the player as either a threat… or a successor.

Sahira Vey — Leader of the Sandriven
Determined, compassionate, fierce.
Opposite of Arikan in every way.
She carries:

- her clan’s traditions

- a dagger passed down generations

- knowledge of the Pirate King’s treaty

## She may be a future companion or romance option.

Namak the Scar-Thirst
A sadistic Oasis Guard captain.
Believes suffering purifies the soul.
Fights like a whirlwind of sand and steel.

---

Eira Dunechild
A young girl with an unnatural affinity for sensing water underground.
Khiz legends call such people Aquaseers.
She could change the fate of the island — if Arikan discovers her.

---

⚔️ QUEST HOOKS

1. “Drops of Power”
   The player infiltrates the Lifewell Citadel disguised as a supplicant to investigate Arikan’s water silos.

---

2. “The Water Peace Treaty”
   Find the original treaty forged by the Pirate King.
   It includes a forgotten clause that could unmake Arikan’s rule entirely.

---

3. “Sandriven Sabotage”
   Assist (or betray) Sahira’s rebels in disrupting Oasis Guard supply chains.

---

4. “Thirst of the People”
   Help a starving village defy Arikan’s water taxes — triggering heavy consequences.

---

5. “The Drip Caves”
   Free prisoners from the most psychologically torturous location in Khiz.
   The escape sequence is one of the tensest early-game experiences.

---

6. “The Oasis of Revelation”
   A major turning point:

- Arikan reveals his personal history

- The player sees how he justifies his tyranny

- You choose to challenge or empathize with him

## This moment defines Khiz’s endgame.

🧭 PLAYER IMPACT
Khiz asks the player a crucial question:
What do you do when morality and survival collide?
This island gives the player:

- exposure to the Pirate King’s humanitarian legacy

- a confrontation with tyranny born from desperation

- a moral crossroads

- a sense of how fragile island life can be

- a deeper understanding of leadership — good and bad

Khiz changes how the player thinks about power.
It plants more seeds for the question looming over the entire story:
“What makes someone worthy of leading the Archipelago?”
