# Yayimi

1. Yayimi — The Western Foothold (Yuma Presence)
   Theme: Occupation, resistance, simmering rebellion
   Inspiration: Colonial frontier, occupied territories
   Core Concept
   Being the farthest west, Yayimi has the strongest Yuma presence.
   They built bunkers, docks, and technological outposts — but the locals hate them.
   Current State

- Yuma tries to “stabilize” the region with tech soldiers and researchers.

- A pirate rebellion has formed to drive Yuma out.

- Neutral citizens are stuck between tyranny and chaos.

Story Themes

- Civil unrest

- Guerrilla warfare

- Propaganda vs truth

- A pirate warlord rising to become a folk hero

---

🌍 GEOGRAPHY & CLIMATE
Yayimi is a cool, windswept island with:

- Rocky coastlines

- Misty forests

- Long, sloping hills ideal for watchtowers

- A central port city (Tarethar) that dominates the western coast

- Scattered fishing villages that dot the shore like fragile teeth

## Its terrain makes it tactically valuable — dense forest cover and elevated cliffs allow perfect vantage points for rebels.

🏴‍☠️ BEFORE THE PIRATE KING (60+ years ago)
Yayimi was a quiet, poor island, frequently raided during the Yuma–Lilan conflicts. Most of its young men were conscripted or abducted; many never returned.
It developed a culture of:

- Stoic endurance

- Distrust of both continents

- Fierce community bonds

## Their fishing industry was modest, sustained by dangerous northern waters.

👑 UNDER THE PIRATE KING (20–10 years ago)
The Pirate King liberated Yayimi early in his rise. He:

- Drove Yuma and Lilan forces out

- Helped rebuild Tarethar’s harbor

- Instituted pirate protection in exchange for modest trade taxes

- Treated Yayimi as the gateway to western freedom

For the first time in generations, Yayimi prospered.
Children grew up believing in the Pirate King as more myth than man — a guardian deity of the sea.

---

⚔️ AFTER THE PIRATE KING (last 10 years)
With his death, Yayimi was the first island Yuma reclaimed because of its proximity.
They arrived with:

- A “peacekeeping fleet”

- Technomancers

- Engineers

- Behavioral reform officers

- Propaganda units

Yuma insists Yayimi “voluntarily rejoined the Republic.”
Locals know otherwise.

---

🛡️ CURRENT POLITICAL STATE

1. Yuma Occupation (Official Rule)
   Yuma runs Yayimi through:

- High Officer Dara Vens, a calculating career soldier

- “Reeducation centers”

- Curfews enforced by mechanized watchposts

- A new naval fort built into Yayimi’s western cliffs

- Taxation that bleeds the island dry

Yuma says they bring “order.”
The people hear “subjugation.”

---

2. The Rebellion (Unofficial Power)
   A charismatic pirate-turned-resistance leader, Captain Marrek Thorn, rallies Yayimi’s people.
   His group — The Tidebreakers — operates from the forests, striking Yuma supply caravans and freeing prisoners.
   Marrek once served under the Pirate King.
   He refuses to let Yayimi forget what freedom felt like.
   The people whisper:
   “If anyone could unite the islands again, it’s Marrek.”
   This makes him a target.

---

3. Civilians (Caught in the Middle)
   Most Yayimi citizens:

- Hate the occupation

- Fear what rebellion will bring

- Remember what chaos looked like before the Pirate King

- Long for stability, not war

You’ll see:

- Families torn between loyalty and survival

- Underground safehouses

- Smugglers helping refugees escape to Klechi

- Yuma officers forcing conscription “for their own protection”

---

🧩 FACTIONS OF YAYIMI

1. Yuma Garrison

- Rigorous

- Disciplined

- Armed with early-stage firearms, shock batons, mech-augmented armor

- Loyal to the Republic above all

Their goal:
Create a western staging point for retaking the entire Archipelago.

---

2. The Tidebreakers (Rebels / Pirates)

- Marrek Thorn as leader

- Symbol: A broken anchor wrapped in sea-blue cloth

- Guerrilla tactics

- Supported informally by Klechi shipwrights

Their whispered motto:
“The sea was ours once — it will be again.”

---

3. The Gray Nets (Neutral Fisherfolk)
   They provide food for both sides to survive.
   They hate the conflict and consider both sides bad for business.
   But beneath their neutrality, they hold a hidden strength:

- They control secret coves

- They know the tides better than military maps

- And they are the ones who remember the Pirate King's final visit

## Their elder, Osha Nayen, knows a dangerous secret about the Pirate King's legacy — something Yuma wants badly.

🔥 KEY LOCATIONS
🛠️ Tarethar (Capital Port)

- Yuma’s main foothold

- Once beautiful; now industrialized and scarred

- A massive Yuma dreadnought dominates the harbor

---

🌲 The Whisperwoods
Dense forest where:

- Tidebreakers hide

- Folk legends say spirits protect the land

- Yuma troops frequently disappear

---

🪨 The Cliffs of Sorrow
Where Yuma executes captured rebels.
Marrek Thorn’s brother was killed here — the moment that sparked his revolution.

---

⚙️ Fort Ashbreaker
A brutalist Yuma base built into a cliffside.
Functions as a prison, armory, and research facility.
Rumor:
Yuma is testing a new weapon here — a prototype meant to “end piracy forever.”

---

🎭 MAJOR NPCS
Marrek Thorn — Pirate Rebel Hero

- Charismatic, grieving, hopeful

- Acts like he doesn’t want power, but everyone knows he could become the next Pirate King

- Wields twin cutlasses infused with storm-energy (a relic of the Pirate King)

He will either be one of the greatest allies the player can gain…
or one of the greatest rivals.

---

Dara Vens — Yuma High Officer

- Controlled, calculating

- Genuinely believes Yuma rule will prevent another age of pirate terror

- Hiding guilt over past war crimes

## She sees Yayimi as the place she can redeem herself — or forever stain her soul.

Osha Nayen — Elder of the Gray Nets

- Gentle

- Wise

- Holds forbidden knowledge about how the Pirate King united the seas

- Knows where the Pirate King's true legacy was hidden before he died

When she finally reveals it to the player…
everything changes.

---

⚔️ QUEST HOOKS

1. “The Spark of Rebellion”
   Help (or betray) Marrek Thorn in a key ambush of Yuma weaponry.
2. “Ashes in the Tide”
   Investigate disappearances in Whisperwoods — leads to arcane experiments.
3. “The Elder’s Burden”
   Osha reveals fragments of the Pirate King's final message.
4. “Fort Ashbreaker Must Fall”
   A major early-game raid or infiltration.
5. “Whose Freedom?”
   A gray-morality quest forcing the player to decide between:

- Supporting rebellion

- Helping innocent families avoid conflict

- Or stabilizing Yayimi through Yuma control

## The world remembers your choice.

🧭 PLAYER IMPACT
Starting in Yayimi lets the player immediately experience:

- What the Pirate King meant to people

- How fragile the Archipelago is now

- The moral grayness of Yuma’s “order”

- How rebellion is both beautiful and tragic
