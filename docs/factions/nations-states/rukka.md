# Rukka

🌑 ISLAND LORE CHAPTER 14: RUKKA
“The Isle of the Drowned Fury”
Status: Radicalized fishman nation seeking vengeance
Tone: Oppressive, stormy, tragic, righteous fury
Themes: Rage born from injustice, generational trauma, extremism, cycles of violence, the cost of being right in the wrong way

---

🌋 GEOGRAPHY & ENVIRONMENT
Where Thult is bright and welcoming, Rukka is bleak and brutal.
Defining features:

- Black volcanic sands

- Razor-sharp coral wastelands

- Jagged reefs where ships are torn to pieces

- Perpetual storm clouds gathering around the central peaks

- Sulfur pools and murky tidal marshes

- Deep trenches riddled with predatory sea life

- Underwater fortresses built from shipwreck debris

## Rukka looks as if the world tried to drown it — and it survived through hatred alone.

🧬 THE PEOPLE OF RUKKA
The Rukkar fishmen evolved harsher adaptations:

- thicker scales

- sharper bone plates

- venom glands

- bioluminescent markings used to intimidate

- more aggressive physiology

They are not inherently evil — they were shaped by cruelty.
Rukkar ideology:
"Forgiveness is a luxury of the unbroken."

---

⏳ BEFORE THE PIRATE KING
Rukka suffered far worse than Thult.
Humans:

- captured them for slavery

- experimented on them

- poisoned their waters

- destroyed their sacred reefs

- forced them to fight each other for amusement

Even Yuma and Lilan, during their expansionist eras, considered Rukka “dangerous creatures” rather than people.
The Rukkar do not forget.
They do not forgive.

---

👑 UNDER THE PIRATE KING
The Pirate King forced an end to fishman slavery across the Archipelago.
But unlike Thult, Rukka did not trust him.
He:

- freed hundreds of Rukkar slaves

- destroyed several trafficking rings

- executed slavers himself

- offered them a seat at his table

But Rukka rejected it.
To them:

- he was still a human

- his mercy came too late

- his compassion could not erase centuries of pain

Only one Rukkar ever fought beside him — a legendary warrior named Marruk Fangfall.
And even he left the crew eventually, believing the Pirate King’s dream was doomed.

---

⚔️ AFTER THE PIRATE KING’S DEATH (Years 1–10)
Rukka hardened further.
They became increasingly:

- isolated

- militant

- radicalized

- driven by old wounds and new fears

They saw the Pirate King’s death as proof that:

- humans cannot be trusted

- peace is a lie

- a new age of Rukkar dominance must begin

- the ocean belongs to its first children

## Their leader emerged from this ideology like a tidal wave of wrath.

🛡️ CURRENT POLITICAL STATE
Rukka is ruled by a Warlord-King known as:
KARZOK THE ABYSS-BORN
A towering, terrifying leviathan of a fishman.
Traits:

- massive eel-like body

- scarred from chains and harpoons

- venomous spines along his arms

- burning bioluminescent eyes

- a voice like grinding stone

He was once a slave.
He broke free.
He killed the crew that owned him.
He swam home and took the throne by force.
His philosophy:
“We asked for mercy. We were drowned.
We asked for peace. We were enslaved.
Now we take what is owed.”
He sees Thult’s attempts at diplomacy as betrayal.
He sees Rukka as the spear of justice.

   Not all Rukkar want war.
   Some:

- are exhausted

5. The Remnants of Marruk Fangfall
   The last warriors loyal to the Pirate King’s vision.
   Small in number.
   Hunted by the Abyssal Guard.
   Clinging to the idea that peace is still possible…

🧩 KEY LOCATIONS
🌩️ Stormbreaker Crag
A massive jagged fortress built into a cliff facing relentless storms.
Home of the warlord.
No human has entered and lived.

---

💀 The Bone Reefs
Shipwreck graveyards where:

- skeletal hulls jut from the water

- eel-like predators lurk

- Rukkar scouts watch from the shadows

## A warning to outsiders.

🕳️ The Abyssal Mouth
A deep pit underwater leading to:

- ancient ruins

- dormant sea monsters

- sacred Rukkar sites

Here, the Tideburn Witches perform rituals.
And something old listens.

---

🔥 The Shattered Village
Ruins of the place Karzok was enslaved from.
He returns there often.
His hatred is born here.

---

🌊 The Twin Currents Strait
The narrow waterway dividing Thult and Rukka.
Its currents shift unpredictably — almost as if responding to the emotional tension between the islands.
This strait may host one of the game’s most dramatic story moments.

---

🎭 MAJOR NPCS
Karzok the Abyss-Born — Warlord of Rukka
A tragic villain, not an evil one.
He wants justice — but cannot tell justice from vengeance anymore.
He respects strength and despises pity.
The player may:

- challenge him

- negotiate with him

- betray him

- or transform him

## His fate shapes the entire Archipelago.

Seryna Coilwind — Tideburn Witch
A prophetic, eerie mystic.
She claims:

- the Pirate King “angered the deep”

- something ancient stirs

- the player is the pivot of fate

## She may help or curse the player.

Tarek of the Fangless
A humble, broken fishman who wants peace.
His story is heartbreaking.
He could be the spark that changes Rukka’s destiny — or he could die in the crossfire.

---

Kadrox and Shyll — Abyssal Twins
Elite siblings who act as Karzok’s enforcers.
One is brutal.
The other is strategic.
Together they are unstoppable.

⚔️ QUEST HOOKS

1. “Voice of the Deep”
   Investigate rituals at the Abyssal Mouth.
   Learn:

- what ancient force the Witches serve

- whether the Pirate King was involved

- the connection between Rukka and a submerged civilization

---

2. “The Fangless Rebellion”
   Help (or suppress) a small resistance within Rukka.
   Your choice determines whether Rukka softens or hardens.

---

3. “The Lost Warrior Marruk”
   Find the legendary Marruk Fangfall.
   He alone can challenge Karzok — or reveal truths about the Pirate King.
   He may also reveal:

- a secret about the Pirate King’s death

- a prophecy tied to the tides

- something world-shaking

## This is a major plot arc.

4. “Twin Currents”
   A dramatic encounter in the strait between Thult and Rukka.
   Choices:

- prevent war

- ignite war

- redirect anger toward a third party

- expose conspirators

- or fail — triggering a massive sea battle

---

5. “The Stormbreaker Throne”
   Face Karzok.
   Not every ending requires killing him.
   Possibilities include:

- defeat

- duel for respect

- forcing negotiation

- shifting his ideology

- or allowing him to fall to ancient forces

---

🧭 PLAYER IMPACT
Rukka is one of the emotional anchors of your entire game.
It forces the player to confront:

- prejudice

- trauma

- systemic injustice

- the consequences of human cruelty

- the difficulty of reconciling peace and vengeance

The player’s choices here echo throughout the world:

- alter Thult

- reshape sea politics

- determine the future of fishman-human relations

- impact global alliances

- and influence how others view the possibility of a new Pirate King

Rukka is rage given form.
And the player must decide:
**Does the tide of fury drown the Vale…
or does a new tide rise?**
