# Busha

14. Busha — Human Fishermen Caught in the Middle
    Theme: Collateral damage, prejudice, survival
    Core Concept
    Busha lies between human and fishmen territories. The island suffers constant raids, retaliations, misunderstandings.
    Traits

- Fishing villages

- Vigilante militias

- Kidnapping cycles between humans and fishmen

Story Themes

- Mediation

- Breaking cycles of hatred

- “Eye for an eye makes the whole sea blind”

- One of the most emotional arcs

---

🐟 ISLAND LORE CHAPTER 15: BUSHA
“The Island Between the Tides”
Status: Human fishing nation, under immense pressure
Tone: Somber, tense, empathetic, morally complex
Themes: Fear vs compassion, cycles of retaliation, survival under oppression, truth distorted by trauma

---

🐚 GEOGRAPHY & ENVIRONMENT
Busha is a modest island with:

- quiet fishing villages

- weather-beaten docks

- rolling coastal hills

- long stretches of rocky shoreline

- small pine forests

- cliffs overlooking the Twin Currents Strait

- shallow bays filled with migratory fish

It is a peaceful, humble place…
or at least it used to be.
Now it feels like a nation always waiting for the next tragedy.

---

👥 THE PEOPLE OF BUSHA
Bushans are:

- hardworking

- humble

- toughened by sea life

- community-centered

But also:

- fearful

- suspicious of fishmen

- victims of misinformation

- exploited by outside powers

Most have never harmed fishmen.
But many hold deep fear — even prejudice — after years of raids, retaliations, and political manipulation.
Their culture centers on:

- fishing

- boat-building

- seamanship

- weather lore

- seasonal festivals

And now:
fear of crossing waters near Rukka.

---

⏳ BEFORE THE PIRATE KING
Busha had a complicated relationship with fishmen.
Sometimes:

- they traded fish and tools

- children played on docks and reefs

- Bushans and Thul’tari cooperated

But other times:

- human poachers attacked Rukka waters

- fishmen retaliated

- villages on Busha suffered losses

- rumors and fear replaced truth

Slavers also operated offshore, using Busha’s ports without the knowledge of most villagers.
Busha became stuck between brutality and misunderstanding.

---

👑 UNDER THE PIRATE KING
The Pirate King brought stability.
He:

- stopped slavers

- punished pirates who attacked fishmen

- enforced naval patrols

- personally negotiated fishing boundaries

- protected Bushan villages

For the first time in centuries, Busha tasted peace.
They held a festival in his honor each year of his rule.

---

⚔️ AFTER THE PIRATE KING’S DEATH (Years 1–10)
That peace shattered instantly.
With the Pirate King gone:

- slavers returned

- extremist Rukkar raids resumed

- Yuma and Lilan warships passed too close

- fishing grounds became contested

- Thult struggled to maintain calm

- rumors spread like wildfire

Bushans now live with:

- constant tension

- unpredictable attacks

- economic hardship

- political neglect

- trauma passed between generations

Some blame Rukka.
Some blame Thult.
Some blame humans.
Some blame everyone.
Busha is the emotional heart of the fishman arc, because it shows the human side of fear and survival.

---

🛡️ CURRENT POLITICAL STATE
Busha is governed loosely — more like a federation of fishing towns than a single nation.
The major power groups:

1. Mayor Serren Holloway of Tidebreak Village
   The de facto leader of Busha’s largest settlement.
   She is:

- compassionate

- exhausted

- trying desperately to prevent war

- constantly pressured by fearful villagers

## She supports Thult’s diplomacy, but her people blame fishmen for their suffering.

2. The Dockwardens
   A militia of fishermen who:

- defend villages

- patrol the coast

- salvage wreckage

- try to keep order

They are brave but undertrained.
Some members are calm and fair.
Others are deeply prejudiced.

---

3. The Harpoon Brotherhood (Extremist Faction)
   A violent, angry group of fishermen who lost family to Rukka raids.
   They believe:

- all fishmen are monsters

- Thult is lying

- war is inevitable

- Busha must strike first

They hold secret meetings.
They stockpile illegal weapons.
They have connections to slavers.
This group is extremely dangerous.

---

4. The Silent Nets (Smuggling Syndicate)
   A group that profits from:

- illegal fishing

- slaver negotiations

- stolen goods

- false “warnings” to stir chaos

They thrive on tension between fishmen and humans.
Stopping them is difficult — many locals rely on their income.

---

5. The Mourning Tide Families
   Dozens of households who have lost loved ones at sea.
   Their grief is weaponized by extremists.
   They don’t hate fishmen — they’re simply broken.
   They become a powerful emotional anchor for the player.

---

🧩 KEY LOCATIONS
🌊 Tidebreak Village (Capital)
Wooden docks, fish markets, creaky houses, storm bells lining the shore.
This is where the player learns the human side of the conflict.

---

🏚️ Breakwater Ruins
A small village destroyed by a Rukka raid five years ago.
No one has rebuilt it.
It is left as a reminder — and as propaganda fuel for extremists.

---

⚓ The Harpoon Hall
A repurposed boathouse where the Harpoon Brotherhood gathers.
Inside:

- harpoons with engraved names

- walls covered with memorials

- propaganda posters

- illegal weapons crates

## This place can ignite a civil war.

🌬️ Whisperwind Point
A cliff overlooking the strait between Rukka and Thult.
Used as both:

- a mourning site

- a lookout post

Here, children tie ribbons for lost sailors.
This is one of the most emotional scenes on the island.

---

🕳️ The Contraband Caves
Sea caves used by the Silent Nets for smuggling slaves, stolen goods, and even fishman captives.
Discovering this is a huge story moment — revealing that Busha is not purely a victim.

---

🌧️ The Storm Chapel
A small shrine where Bushans pray for safe seas.
This is where the player confronts the island’s spiritual heart — and its collective grief.

---

🎭 MAJOR NPCS
Mayor Serren Holloway
Tired but hopeful.
She needs help, not judgment.
She is torn between:

- empathy

- duty

- fear

- pressure from extremists

## Her fate affects Busha’s future for generations.

Garron Pike — Leader of the Harpoon Brotherhood
Charismatic. Broken. Dangerous.
He lost two children to Rukka.
He is a tragic villain — not evil, but consumed by trauma.

---

Marra Tidewatch — Dockwarden Captain
Reasonable, level-headed, deeply conflicted.
Wants peace with Thult.
Terrified of Rukka.
Suspicious of outsiders.
Could be a powerful ally.

---

Tulen “Softshell” Finn — Smuggler
Cowardly, witty, manipulative.
A source of comic relief… but very morally gray.
He knows too much about the Silent Nets.

---

Aelis Kesh (from Thult)
Her arrival in Busha may be one of the most emotional scenes in the game.
She wants peace.
Busha… may not be ready.

---

⚔️ QUEST HOOKS

1. “The Raid That Wasn’t”
   A boat sinks off the coast.
   Everyone assumes Rukka did it.
   But investigation may reveal:

- pirates

- smugglers

- natural storm

- or human sabotage

## This quest challenges the player’s assumptions.

2. “Harpoons in the Dark”
   Stop (or support) the Harpoon Brotherhood’s escalation.
   They plan to:

- attack Thult

- provoke Rukka

- fake a fishman raid

## Your choices alter the entire fishman arc.

3. “The Silent Nets Unmasked”
   Expose the smuggling ring.
   This reveals:

- corruption in Busha

- their role in destabilizing Thult’s peace efforts

- ties to outside nations

---

4. “Ashes of Breakwater”
   Help survivors return to the destroyed village and confront their grief.
   This is one of the most emotional and human quests in the game.

---

5. “Meeting at Whisperwind Point”
   A high-stakes diplomacy attempt with Thult representatives.
   Depending on previous choices, this could end in:

- hope

- violence

- betrayal

- or reconciliation

## This scene becomes a turning point for the entire Archipelago.

🧭 PLAYER IMPACT
Busha is where the player learns the hardest lesson:
Even good people can become enemies when fear owns them.
This island shows:

- humans aren’t purely victims

- fishmen aren’t uniformly justified

- trauma shapes nations

- peace requires sacrifice

- understanding is harder than combat

Busha becomes a bridge — or a breaking point — depending on player choices.
Your decisions here will shape:

- Thult

- Rukka

- sea politics

- Yuma’s stance

- Lilan’s propaganda

- the player’s moral reputation

This is the emotional, human center of the fishman arc.
