# Kirkia

9. Kirkia — Jungle of Untamed Nature
   Theme: Primordial power, ancient spirits, lost temples
   Core Concept
   A massive jungle island where wildlife, plants, and spirits overshadow civilization.
   Traits

- Ruins of forgotten peoples

- Intelligent beasts

- Tribes divided between harmony and domination

Story Themes

- “Tame or be eaten”

- Spirit trials

- Lost magic

---

🌍 GEOGRAPHY & CLIMATE
Kirkia is a massive jungle island overflowing with life to an unnerving degree.
Features:

- Towering rainforest canopy that blocks out most sunlight

- Vines thicker than ropes

- Rivers that twist like serpents

- Tremendous waterfalls dropping into mist-crowned basins

- Bioluminescent groves active at night

- Hidden ancient ruins wrapped in roots

- Wildlife that borders on mythic in its ferocity

The jungle feels… aware.
Travelers say the trees watch.
The earth shifts.
And trails that existed yesterday vanish today.

---

⏳ BEFORE THE PIRATE KING
Kirkia was never conquered — not by Yuma, not by Lilan, not even by the Archipelago’s own.
Attempts at colonization resulted in:

- entire expeditions vanishing

- soldiers being pulled into the undergrowth

- weapons rusting overnight

- camps swallowed by vines

The native tribes lived in harmony with the land:

- hunter societies

- spirit worship

- shifting settlements

- rituals to placate forest gods

They warned outsiders:
“Do not try to own Kirkia. It already has an owner.”

---

👑 UNDER THE PIRATE KING
The Pirate King visited Kirkia only once… and returned with scars on his arms shaped like vine burn marks.
He:

- made no attempt to claim the island

- left offerings to its spirits

- befriended a tribe elder

- warned his crews to respect Kirkia

In return, the tribes honored his authority.
For a decade, the jungle remained at peace — or as peaceful as Kirkia can be.

---

⚔️ AFTER THE PIRATE KING’S DEATH (Years 1–10)
When the Pirate King died, something shifted.
The jungle became:

- Wilder

- Hungrier

- More aggressive

- Unnaturally overgrown

The tribes fractured:

- Some embraced the chaos

- Some resisted it

- Some disappeared into the deep jungle

- Some formed alliances with creatures once thought mythical

And a new phenomenon emerged:
The Greenblight
A creeping corruption that:

- mutates plants

- twists animals into monsters

- infects humans with primal rage

- spreads through the jungle like a disease

Some say it is nature reclaiming power.
Others whisper:
“The Pirate King took something from Kirkia… and now it wants it back.”

---

🛡️ CURRENT POLITICAL STATE

1. The Rootbound Sovereignty (Aggressive Faction)
   Led by Chieftain Varo Thornskin, a towering warrior covered in bark-like armor.
   They believe:

- the jungle should rule

- humans must become one with the wild

- outsiders should be destroyed

They embrace Greenblight willingly, gaining:

- enhanced strength

- regenerative abilities

- primal senses

## At a terrible cost.

2. The Dawnwalkers (Peaceful Faction)
   Led by Elder Seluvi, one of the few remaining spiritual leaders.
   They:

- seek balance

- try to heal the Greenblight

- protect travelers (begrudgingly)

- preserve ancient rituals

## They knew the Pirate King personally and guarded one of his secrets.

3. The Beastmenders
   A neutral faction of herbalists and beast tamers.
   They:

- treat wounded fauna

- ride giant jungle creatures

- understand the Greenblight better than anyone

- want to prevent total ecological collapse

## Their sanctuary is one of the few safe places on the island.

4. The Deepwild Horde (Mutated Threat)
   Creatures twisted by Greenblight into:

- grotesque beasts

- semi-sentient predators

- hive-minded swarms

- plant-animal hybrids

They are not a faction — they are a danger.
And they are evolving.

---

🧩 KEY LOCATIONS
🌳 The Verdant Maw
A colossal opening in the canopy where the jungle drops into a deep chasm.
It roars at night.
Not with wind — with something alive.

---

💚 The Blightcore Grove
The heart of the Greenblight infection.
Plants pulse like veins.
Pools glow with sickly green light.
Beasts prowl with unnatural fury.
This place corrupts anything that stays too long.

---

🏹 Dawnwalker Hollow
A serene valley protected by ancient wards.
Full of:

- calm animals

- medicinal herbs

- murals depicting Kirkia’s history

- a shrine visited by the Pirate King

## Elder Seluvi lives here.

🐾 Beastmender Refuge
Built on the back of a colossal tortoise that roams the jungle.
A moving sanctuary of:

- cages

- herbal labs

- beast nurseries

- apprentice tamers

## The tortoise is centuries old… and possibly sentient.

⚡ The Stormroot Spire
A gigantic tree that rises above the entire island.
Lightning strikes it constantly but never harms it.
At the top is a ruin that predates all known cultures — and contains carvings resembling the Pirate King’s sigil.

---

🎭 MAJOR NPCS
Varo Thornskin — Chieftain of the Rootbound
Huge, scarred, terrifying.
He believes he is chosen by Kirkia itself.
Thinks the Archipelago needs a “return to primal truth.”
Will try to recruit the player.
Or try to kill them.

---

Elder Seluvi — Spiritual Leader of the Dawnwalkers
Gentle, wise, patient.
Carries:

- knowledge of ancient rituals

- memories of the Pirate King’s visit

- a prophecy involving a “Stormborne One”

## She sees potential in the player… or danger.

Nahri Leafblood — Beastmender Warrior
A fierce hunter capable of bonding with jungle creatures.
Could become a powerful companion.
She’s hiding a mutation she tries desperately to control.

---

The Verdant Titan
A towering Moss-Beast rumored to be:

- guardian spirit

- corrupted elder god

- remnant of the island’s first civilization

The Pirate King wounded it deeply during his visit.
It remembers him.
And it senses something familiar in the player.

---

⚔️ QUEST HOOKS

1. “Call of the Greenblight”
   Investigate the spreading corruption.
   Discover that something (or someone) is accelerating it.

---

2. “Beast of the Maw”
   A massive predator threatens both factions.
   Hunting or taming it unlocks a major ally or weapon.

---

3. “Stormroot Secret”
   Climb the enchanted tree and uncover:

- carvings tied to the Pirate King

- evidence he sealed something within Kirkia

- a clue to the main storyline

## This is a key lore moment.

4. “The Rootbound Choice”
   Varo Thornskin offers the player Greenblight blessings:

- strength

- speed

- endurance

…at risk of corruption.
A major moral/roleplay choice.

---

5. “Requiem of the First Jungle”
   Seluvi reveals the truth:
   “Kirkia is not a place. It is a force.
   And it remembers who tried to rule it.”
   She may show the player visions of the Pirate King’s past.

---

🧭 PLAYER IMPACT
Kirkia teaches the player:

- nature can be as political as civilization

- power not balanced becomes corruption

- the Pirate King was connected to ancient forces

- the world is far older than its empires

- some islands may not be ruled — they must be respected

This island tests the player’s:

- morality

- resilience

- leadership

- relationship with primal or mystical forces

It pushes the narrative toward the bigger question:
**What did the Pirate King truly do on these islands…
and what unfinished business now stirs?**
