# Attributes, Training and derived statistics

Might maps to Strength, Agility to Dexterity, Resolve to Constitution, Wit to Intelligence, Instinct to Wisdom and Spirit to Charisma. Modifier=floor((score−10)/2): 9 gives −1; 10 and 11 give 0; 12 gives +1. Normal attribute cap is 20 unless an explicit feature increases it. No paired-check calculation remains.

Training Bonus (TB)=2+floor((level−1)/4): +2 at 1–4, +3 at 5–8, +4 at 9–12, +5 at 13–16, +6 at 17–20. Training contributes TB once. Expertise contributes 2×TB and requires an already trained skill/tool; multiple Expertise sources never stack. Saving throws cannot gain Expertise.

Skill modifier=attribute modifier + 0/TB/2TB for untrained/trained/Expertise + explicit distinct bonuses. Save modifier=attribute modifier + TB if trained in that save + applicable feature bonuses. Passive Perception=10+Perception modifier, with +5/−5 for relevant advantage/disadvantage. A tool check uses the GM-selected attribute plus tool Training/Expertise. Applicable skill and tool together add TB once and grant advantage under SRD tool rules; they do not add two Training bonuses.

Weapon attack=d20 + Might modifier for melee/thrown melee or Agility modifier for ranged + TB if trained + explicit bonuses. Finesse chooses Might or Agility for both attack and damage. Damage=weapon dice + the same attribute modifier + explicit damage bonuses. The Light extra attack omits a positive attribute modifier unless Two-Weapon Fighting applies; a negative modifier still applies. Unarmed attack uses Might+TB and damage 1+Might modifier, minimum zero. Grapple/shove instead force a Might or Agility save, target's choice, DC 8+TB+Might modifier, with ordinary size/reach constraints. Critical hits double rolled damage dice, not flat modifiers. Long-range attacks have disadvantage; beyond long range is invalid; total cover blocks targeting.

Spell attack=casting modifier+TB. Spell save DC=8+casting modifier+TB. Initiative=Agility modifier+explicit features; Alert adds TB once. Base walking Speed=30 feet. Distinct additive speed bonuses stack, but alternate movement speeds share movement already spent and do not add to walking Speed. Difficult Terrain costs one extra foot per foot regardless of overlapping sources. Standing from Prone costs half Speed. Forced movement does not spend Speed or provoke Opportunity Attacks unless explicitly stated.

Maximum Health=sum of fixed level contributions using current Resolve modifier + explicit feature bonuses. Omen Diver adds its full Tidecaller level beginning at 3. Hit Dice capacity equals level. Guard uses one selected legal base: unarmored 10+Agility; Reaver without armor 10+Agility+Resolve; Omen Diver without armor 10+Agility+Spirit; or worn armor's formula. Add an eligible trained Shield once and distinct bonuses. Untrained armor may be worn but imposes disadvantage on Might/Agility d20 tests and prevents spellcasting. An untrained Shield provides no Guard bonus and still occupies a hand. Heavy armor's unmet Might requirement reduces Speed by 10 feet. No two armors or combining armored/unarmored formulas.

Recalculate after attribute, level, equipment, choice and effect changes, preserving expenditures. Example: level-five Gunslinger with Agility 16, Archery and trained longbow has attack +8 (3+3+2), damage 1d8+3 and Guard 15 in studded leather. Doctor with Resolve 14 has Health maximum 10 at level 1 and 17 at level 2; Resolve increasing to 16 at level 2 makes maximum 19 and increases current by 2 while preserving damage.
