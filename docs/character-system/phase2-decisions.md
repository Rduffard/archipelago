# Phase 2 approved decisions

## Captain's Credit timing — 2026-11-08

The user selected **off turn, once per round** after reviewing the mechanical difference between the conflicting Phase 1 ability text and acceptance example. This decision supersedes the own-turn wording in the historical Phase 1 baseline.

Once per combat round, outside your turn, when one ally you can see within 60 feet succeeds on an attack roll or saving throw while inside your active Squall, grant that ally temporary Health equal to your Training Bonus plus Instinct modifier (minimum one).

The grant does not stack with existing temporary Health. It does not restore currency, spend another Squall use, or grant an Action. The GM confirms sight, range, zone membership, success, external turn and combat round. Recorded combat identity, round number and success event prevent duplicate grants, reused successes and backward round submissions. A new combat has a separate limit. Squall duration and other feature clauses remain unchanged.
