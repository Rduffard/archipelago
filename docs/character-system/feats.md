## Alert — `feat:alert`

Category origin;minimumLevel 1;repeatable no.

Origin Feat You gain the following benefits. Initiative Training. When you roll Initiative, you can add your Training Bonus to the roll. Initiative Swap. Immediately after you roll Initiative, you can swap your Initiative with the Initiative of one willing ally in the same combat. You can't make this swap if you or the ally has the Incapacitated condition.

## Magic Initiate — `feat:magic-initiate`

Category origin;minimumLevel 1;repeatable yes,subject to rules.

Origin Feat You gain the following benefits. Two Cantrips. You learn two cantrips of your choice from the Doctor, Druid, or Shipwright spell list. Wit, Instinct, or Spirit is your spellcasting ability for this feat's spells (choose when you select this feat). Level 1 Spell. Choose a level 1 spell from the same list you selected for this feat's cantrips. You always have that spell prepared. You can cast it once without a spell slot, and you regain the ability to cast it in that way when you finish a Long Rest. You can also cast the spell using any spell slots you have. Spell Change. Whenever you gain a new level, you can replace one of the spells you chose for this feat with a different spell of the same level from the chosen spell list. Repeatable. You can take this feat more than once, but you must choose a different spell list each time.

## Savage Attacker — `feat:savage-attacker`

Category origin;minimumLevel 1;repeatable no.

Origin Feat You've trained to deal particularly damaging strikes. Once per turn when you hit a target with a weapon, you can roll the weapon's damage dice twice and use either roll against the target.

## Skilled — `feat:skilled`

Category origin;minimumLevel 1;repeatable yes,subject to rules.

Origin Feat You gain Training in any combination of three skills or tools of your choice. Repeatable. You can take this feat more than once.

## Ability Score Improvement — `feat:ability-score-improvement`

Category general;minimumLevel 4;repeatable yes,subject to rules.

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+) Increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. This feat can't increase an ability score above 20. Repeatable. You can take this feat more than once.

## Grappler — `feat:grappler`

Category general;minimumLevel 4;repeatable no.

General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Might or Agility 13+) You gain the following benefits. Ability Score Increase. Increase your Might or Agility score by 1, to a maximum of 20. Punch and Grab. When you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike as part of the Attack action on your turn, you can use both the Damage and the Grapple option. You can use this benefit only once per turn. Attack Advantage. You have Advantage on attack rolls against a creature Grappled by you. Fast Wrestler. You don't have to spend extra movement to move a creature Grappled by you if the creature is your size or smaller.

## Great Weapon Fighting — `feat:great-weapon-fighting`

Category fighting-style;minimumLevel 1;repeatable no.

Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature) When you roll damage for an attack you make with a Melee weapon that you are holding with two hands, you can treat any 1 or 2 on a damage die as a 3. The weapon must have the Two-Handed or Versatile property to gain this benefit.

Fighting Style Feat (Prerequisite: Fighting Style Feature) When you make an extra attack as a result of using a weapon that has the Light property, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of that attack if you aren't already adding it to the damage.

## Selection and state rules

ASI grants +2 to one attribute or +1 to two distinct attributes, total exactly two, cap 20, repeatable. Grappler requires level 4 and Might or Agility 13, plus its printed chosen +1. Epic Boon increases only its chosen attribute by +1 with cap 30; it does not increase every score's cap automatically.

Available fighting styles are Archery, Defense, Great Weapon Fighting and Two-Weapon Fighting. Navigator/Warden may instead choose the printed cantrip alternative: two Druid/Doctor cantrips respectively, using the parent casting attribute, with one replacement per parent Calling level. This adds no Druid Calling.

Magic Initiate selects Doctor, Druid or Shipwright list, casting attribute Wit/Instinct/Spirit, two distinct cantrips and one level-one spell. Repeat only with a different list. Each grant has its own free-cast counter, one per Long Rest; those spells do not consume ordinary preparation count. The complete Druid-access subset needed here is explicitly listed in spells.md. Skilled chooses three distinct untrained skills/tools. Alert initiative swap requires a willing conscious ally. Savage Attacker rerolls weapon dice only, not Sneak Attack/spell-rider dice, once per turn.

Store limited uses under the feat source grant and clause. Boon of Fate uses its printed range, Reaction and Short/Long Rest limit. Spell Recall checks a d4 against a level-one-through-four slot expended on casting; a match prevents that expenditure, never creates an extra slot. All boon target, action, damage and recovery text remains binding. The feat list is closed for this edition; unavailable non-SRD feats are not unimplemented slots.
