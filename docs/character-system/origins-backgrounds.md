Cultural language IDs are first-edition technical identifiers, not claims of distinct established linguistic families. Common is automatic, plus the origin's cultural language and one standard choice. Standard choices: all 18 cultural IDs, Common Sign, Draconic, Dwarvish, Elvish, Giant, Gnomish, Goblin, Halfling and Orc. Exotic Abyssal, Celestial, Deep Speech, Infernal, Primordial, Sylvan and Undercommon require a feature permitting any language or recorded GM approval. Thieves' Cant requires its feature grant. Languages give no combat bonuses; replace duplicates with another legal selection.

## New Archipelago background designs

Each grants +2 to one listed attribute OR +1 to two distinct listed attributes, total +2; two skills; one tool; one Origin feat; and its gear OR 50 GP. Combined with the origin's +1, the total attribute budget is +3. Do not also apply a 5E +2/+1 background package. Creation cap is 17. Resolve duplicate background skills after Calling selections by choosing any untrained skill; duplicate tools may become another mundane tool. Custom background is universally available.
