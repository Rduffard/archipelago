# Player Identity

Sanguine Archipelago characters should feel defined by what they do under pressure and how they learned to survive the world.

## Identity Sources

Player identity comes from:

- origin
- calling
- Attributes
- Core Checks
- Specialties
- reputation
- equipment and relics
- wounds and consequences

## Core Checks vs Specialties

Core Checks communicate capability.

Specialties communicate style.

Two characters can both have high Guard:

- one has `Brace` and becomes an anchor for allies
- one has no defensive Specialty but uses Guard as raw personal survival

That distinction is central to the progression model.

## Good Identity Signals

A good mechanic should suggest fiction immediately:

- `Jury-Rig`: survives through ugly repairs and impossible improvisation.
- `Suppress`: controls space by making movement dangerous.
- `Attune`: listens to relics and haunted places before they speak aloud.

If a player can describe a Specialty as a behavior rather than a number, it is doing its job.
