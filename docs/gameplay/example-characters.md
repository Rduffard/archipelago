# Example Characters

These examples show the intended relationship between Attributes, Core Checks, Specialties, faction identity, and narrative role.

## Yuma Linebreaker

Identity: disciplined soldier trained to hold imperial routes under impossible pressure.

Likely emphasis:

- Attributes: `Might`, `Resolve`
- Core Checks: `Guard`, `Tactics`, `Endurance`, `Presence`
- Specialties: `Brace`, `Command`, `Suppress`
- Reputation: strong `Yuma Republic`, wary `Rebel Movements`, tense `Free Captains`

Play pattern:

The Linebreaker protects space, creates order, and turns panic into formation. Their power creates suspicion in anti-imperial spaces because their training looks like occupation.

## Lilin Shrine Negotiator

Identity: ritual mediator who treats spirits, oaths, and institutions as living relationships.

Likely emphasis:

- Attributes: `Spirit`, `Resolve`, `Instinct`
- Core Checks: `Grace`, `Focus`, `Ward`, `Omen`
- Specialties: `Attune`, `Read`, `Bargain`
- Reputation: strong `Lilin Empire` or `Faith Orders`, complex `Guild Consortium`

Play pattern:

The Negotiator converts unseen pressure into terms the party can understand. They do not simply cast spells; they ask what the place remembers and what must be honored.

## Free Captain Cutter

Identity: fast, reputation-forward raider who wins by movement, threat, and escape routes.

Likely emphasis:

- Attributes: `Agility`, `Instinct`, `Spirit`
- Core Checks: `Skirmish`, `Reflex`, `Pressure`, `Scouting`
- Specialties: `Cut`, `Feint`, `Board`
- Reputation: strong `Free Captains`, hostile `Yuma Republic`, situational `Underworld Networks`

Play pattern:

The Cutter changes tempo. They create openings, punish hesitation, and make every room feel like a deck with exits.

## Guild Salvage Factor

Identity: institutional problem-solver who moves through craft access, permits, repairs, and favors.

Likely emphasis:

- Attributes: `Wit`, `Resolve`
- Core Checks: `Patch`, `Salvage`, `Fabricate`, `Broker`
- Specialties: `Jury-Rig`, `Calibrate`, `Bargain`
- Reputation: strong `Guild Consortium`, useful `Noble Courts`, watched by `Underworld Networks`

Play pattern:

The Factor solves impossible logistical problems and turns institutional access into tactical leverage. Their weakness is dependency on systems that can be corrupted, seized, or politically compromised.
